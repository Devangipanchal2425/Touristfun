Manali: The Himalayan Gateway

Adventure Awaits in Manali

Manali is a high-altitude Himalayan resort town known for its cool climate and snow-capped mountains. From the spiritual vibes of Hadimba Temple to the adrenaline rush at Solang Valley, Manali is the ultimate retreat for couples and adventure seekers alike.

Best Time to Visit

October to June (For Snow: Dec-Feb)

Top Attractions

Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley, Old Manali Cafe Hopping

Trip Highlights

• Paragliding and Skiing in Solang Valley
• Drive through the majestic Atal Tunnel
• Exploring the local Tibetan Market
